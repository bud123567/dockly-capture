About this app

Turn something on your screen into something easy to explain. Dockly Capture brings screenshot editing, text recognition, and a floating reference image into one native workbench.

  • Capture an area, a window, or your main display
  • Add arrows, rectangles, highlights, and text
  • Cover sensitive details with opaque redaction
  • Crop, undo edits, and save or copy flattened PNGs
  • Recognize text on-device and pin images above other windows

What’s new

App privacy

Images stay on your Mac. Nothing uploads automatically. Session history holds up to 12 originals in memory; Save exports the edited image.
