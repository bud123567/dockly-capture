About this app

A browser on one side. Your notes on the other. Dockly Snap makes room for the way you work, with quick window layouts that keep your hands on the keyboard.

  • Snap windows into halves, quarters, and thirds
  • Arrange windows with Control + Option shortcuts
  • Drag to screen edges with a placement preview
  • Move windows between displays and restore their previous size
