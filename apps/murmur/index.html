About this app

Your existing Murmur, prepared as a separate Dockly edition. Dictate into your workflow, clean up spoken punctuation, and keep your attention on what you want to say.

  • Hold Right Option to talk, or use tap-to-lock
  • See live transcription in a floating voice panel
  • Use spoken punctuation and custom replacements
  • Review and copy recent transcripts from local history
  • Choose on-device Apple Speech or an optional cloud engine

What’s new

Version 0.1.0First development preview

Dockly edition with separate settings, a visible setup window, strictly on-device Apple Speech, and safer clipboard restoration.

App privacy

Apple Speech stays on-device. Choosing a cloud engine sends audio to that provider; optional AI cleanup sends transcript text. API keys stay in macOS Keychain. Local history can be cleared.
